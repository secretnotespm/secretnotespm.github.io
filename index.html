How it works

Private by design

Notes are encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM. Every note gets its own 256-bit key from the browser's random source, plus a unique random salt and nonce, and the key is strengthened with PBKDF2-SHA-256 before it becomes the AES key.

Share links safely

The encrypted payload is stored in the URL fragment after #, which browsers do not send to the web server in HTTP requests. The link never carries the key, so a link that leaks in a browser history, a chat log, or a screenshot is still unreadable. Send the key to the recipient through a separate channel.

Privacy

SecretNotes does not upload or store your note. Keep a copy of the link and the key: if you lose them, the note cannot be recovered. Use a modern browser with Web Crypto support and HTTPS.
